Privacy Policy

Security & Data

1. Data Collection

MailWeave is a read-only email visualization client. We use the Google Gmail API to fetch your email threads and messages for the sole purpose of rendering them in our threaded tree-branch view.

2. Google OAuth Scope

To function, MailWeave requests the gmail.readonly scope. This allows the application to read your messages but does not allow it to send, delete, or modify any of your emails.

3. Data Storage & Security

MailWeave follows a privacy-first architecture. We do not store your emails on any external servers. All data is processed locally on your Android device. Your authentication tokens are stored securely using the Android Keystore via flutter_secure_storage, ensuring they are encrypted and inaccessible to other apps.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information or email content to any third parties. Your data remains exclusively on your device.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
